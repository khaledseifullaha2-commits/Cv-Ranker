Responsibilities & Context
Job Location: Halua Upazila, Jamalpur
Job Responsibility:
Organize field team activities aimed at promoting the participation in site level
activities of all community sub-groups, with a specific focus on those groups generally
facing additional barriers such as women & girls as well as people with disabilities.
Support the SMO in designing a capacity building plan for families living in designated
collective sites.
Support the SMO in rolling out a community-based complaint and feedback mechanism
at site level.
Keep a up-to-date record of community groups, governance structures, population
representatives and other community leaders in the area.
Assist the SMO in maintaining relationships between service providers, national
authorities and the different community governance structures in designated collective
sites.
In coordination with the Needs and Population Monitoring (NPM) and other relevant
actors, support site level assessments and report challenges with service provision;
Assist the SMO in keeping abreast of all community governance issues in the sites.
Assist the SMO in anticipating constraints to project implementation and to work out
solutions.
Remain well informed of the most up to date information related to the services
provided by international and national humanitarian partners as well as relevant
national authorities.
Attend relevant coordination meetings and clearly relay information to stakeholders on
site management activities as instructed by the SMO. Prepare and share meeting
minutes accordingly.
Ensure that noticeboards and other communication materials used at site level contain
up-to-date information on services available and how to access them.
Control the quality of data collected by field assistant and ensure proper information
management. Prepare preliminary analysis of the data collected by Field assistant and
related activity reports
Promote adherence to appropriate safety and security procedures related to the
protection of staff, its assets and operations. Promote awareness and compliance of
all team members regarding security information and procedures.
Prepare relevant finance and procurement documents related to above mentioned
activities and keep the records of all expenses carried out by the team in relation to
those.
Any other duties as may be assigned by the supervisor.
